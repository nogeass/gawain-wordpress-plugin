= [EN] Turn Product Images Into Promotional Videos — Automatically =

[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5cAoG0JBQY]

If you sell products on WooCommerce, you already know the challenge: customers want to see products in motion, but producing videos for every SKU takes time, budget, and creative skill most store owners simply don't have.

**Gawain AI solves this in one click.**

Upload a product image. Gawain AI extracts the concept, writes the copy, composes the scenes, adds animation and BGM — and delivers a ready-to-use promotional video. No editing. No templates. No manual work.

**Works for your industry:**

* Cosmetics & Beauty — Skincare, makeup, and wellness products with mood-driven visuals and benefit-focused copy
* Fashion & Apparel — Seasonal collections, styling suggestions, and trend-forward creative, auto-generated per product
* Pet Supplies — Playful, safe, and lovable product presentations that resonate with pet owners
* Interior & Lifestyle Goods — Lifestyle-scene videos that help customers imagine products in their everyday spaces
* Software & SaaS / IT Products — Generate videos from UI screenshots, device mockups, or product packaging images to promote digital products in your WooCommerce store
* Food & Beverage — (Japanese-language optimized) Fresh, appetizing, and texture-rich visuals for food and drink products
* Media & Publishing — Product videos for merchandise, branded goods, books, and physical products sold through your WooCommerce store

= What Gawain AI Does =

**Fully automated video pipeline**

From a single product image, Gawain AI automatically handles everything:

* Concept extraction (e.g. cosmetics: "radiant x hydrating x summer glow" / fashion: "seasonal x styling x trend-forward" / pet: "playful x safe x adorable" / interior: "minimal x cozy x everyday living")
* Scene composition and cut planning (opening → product intro → usage → closing)
* Copywriting and text overlay
* Video editing and animation compositing
* BGM and effect optimization

**Daily creative rotation**

Fresh videos with different angles and narratives every day. Perfect for social media scheduling and ad campaigns — eliminate "running out of content."

**Brand-preserving AI (industry-first)**

Your product's exact shape, color, and texture are preserved in every frame. Unlike template-based tools that distort or genericize your products, Gawain AI keeps your brand identity intact.

**Native WooCommerce integration**

Generate, preview, deploy, and manage videos directly from your WooCommerce admin panel. Display videos on product pages using the [gawain_videos] shortcode — no theme file editing required.

**Multilingual support**

English, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese — built for international storefronts and inbound markets.

= How to Get Started (4 Steps) =

1. Install and activate the Gawain AI plugin
2. Go to Settings and enable "External processing"
3. Open the Videos tab on any product and click "Generate"
4. Preview your video, then click "Deploy" to display it on the product page

= Plugin Features =

* One-click AI video generation from WooCommerce product images
* Video carousel widget on product pages ([gawain_videos] shortcode)
* Deploy / undeploy videos without editing theme files
* Admin dashboard for generation, preview, deployment, and deletion
* Multilingual UI: English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese

= [한국어] 상품 이미지 1장으로 AI가 홍보 동영상을 전자동 생성 =

WooCommerce에서 상품을 판매하고 있다면, 이제 이런 고민은 필요 없습니다.
"동영상을 만들고 싶지만 시간이 없다. 외주는 비용이 너무 많이 든다. 편집 기술도 없다."

**가웨인 AI가 이 모든 과제를 원클릭으로 해결합니다.**

상품 페이지의 이미지를 읽어들여, 컨셉 설계부터 씬 구성·텍스트·BGM까지 모든 것을 완전 자동으로 완성합니다.

= 지원 업종 =

**뷰티·코스메틱·헬스케어**
스킨케어, 메이크업, 서플리먼트 등 성분·사용감·분위기를 비주얼로 어필하는 동영상을 매일 자동 생성.

**패션·어패럴**
시즌 컬렉션, 스타일링 제안, 트렌드 소구를 상품별로 자동 대량 생산. SNS·광고 소재 제작이 한결 쉬워집니다.

**반려동물 용품**
귀여움과 안전성을 겸비한 반려동물 상품만의 비주얼 소구 동영상. 반려동물 오너의 마음을 사로잡는 콘텐츠를 자동 생성합니다.

**인테리어·잡화·생활용품**
AI가 '일상에 어울리는' 장면을 연출. 고객이 상품을 일상 속에서 사용하는 이미지를 상품 페이지에서 전달합니다.

**소프트웨어·SaaS·IT 제품**
UI 스크린샷, 디바이스 목업, 패키지 이미지를 소재로 디지털 제품·라이선스 상품의 홍보 동영상을 자동 생성합니다.

**미디어·출판·브랜드 굿즈**
서적, 브랜드 굿즈, 오리지널 상품 등 WooCommerce에서 판매 중인 물판 상품의 홍보 동영상을 자동 생성합니다.

= 가웨인 AI로 할 수 있는 것 =

**완전 자동 동영상 생성 파이프라인**

컨셉 추출부터 최종 동영상까지 모든 공정을 AI가 담당:

* 컨셉 추출 (예: 코스메틱 "빛남 × 촉촉함 × 여름의 투명감" / 패션 "시즌 × 스타일링 × 트렌드" / 반려동물 "귀여움 × 안전 × 놀이")
* 씬 구성·컷 분할 (오프닝 → 상품 소개 → 사용 장면 → 클로징)
* 텍스트 생성·카피 연출
* 영상 편집·애니메이션 합성
* BGM·이펙트 최적화

**일일 크리에이티브 로테이션**

매일 다른 각도에서 동영상을 자동 생성. SNS 게시나 광고 소재의 '콘텐츠 부족' 문제를 해결합니다.

**상품 형태를 유지한 채 생성 (업계 최초)**

브랜드가 소중히 여기는 상품의 형태·색·질감을 AI가 정확하게 재현합니다.

= 이런 분께 최적입니다 =

* **뷰티·코스메틱·헬스케어 브랜드** — 전담 동영상 팀 없이 매일 새로운 상품 동영상을 SNS에 게시하고 싶은 분
* **패션·어패럴 스토어** — 신상품별·컬렉션별로 동영상을 대량 생산해 상품 페이지 CVR을 개선하고 싶은 분
* **반려동물 용품점** — 반려동물 오너의 구매 의욕을 높이는 동영상을 저비용으로 만들고 싶은 분
* **인테리어·잡화·생활용품점** — 라이프스타일 장면으로 상품 매력을 전달하고 싶은 분
* **소프트웨어·SaaS·IT 제품 판매 사업자** — 목업이나 스크린샷에서 디지털 상품 홍보 동영상을 만들고 싶은 분
* **미디어·출판·굿즈 판매 사업자** — WooCommerce에서 판매 중인 물판 상품 소개 동영상을 손쉽게 대량 제작하고 싶은 분

= 사용 방법 (4단계) =

1. 플러그인을 설치하고 활성화
2. 설정 화면에서 '외부 처리'를 켜기
3. 상품 목록의 '동영상' 탭에서 '생성' 클릭
4. 미리보기 확인 후 '배포'로 상품 페이지에 표시

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/gawain-ai-video` directory, or install directly via the WordPress Plugins screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.
3. WooCommerce must be installed and active.
4. Go to **Settings > Gawain AI** and enable "External processing" to start generating videos.
5. Navigate to any product and open the **Videos** tab to generate your first video.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this plugin require WooCommerce? =

Yes. Gawain AI is built for WooCommerce stores. WooCommerce must be installed and active. If WooCommerce is not detected, the plugin will display a notice and disable video generation features (the settings page remains accessible under the Tools menu).

= What types of product images work best? =

Clear, high-resolution product images on a plain or simple background produce the best results. The AI works with standard product photography across all supported industries — physical products, packaged goods, software box art, device mockups, and more.

= Can I use the generated videos in paid advertising? =

Yes. All videos generated by Gawain AI are commercial-use ready and can be used directly in social media ads, search ads, and storefront promotions.

= What languages are supported? =

The plugin UI supports English, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese. Video content language is optimized based on your site's language settings.

= Is my product data shared with third parties? =

Product data is only transmitted to the Gawain AI service (gawain.nogeass.com) when you explicitly enable "External processing" in settings and manually trigger video generation. Data is not shared with any other third party. See the External Service section for details.

= Can I remove a video from my product page? =

Yes. Use the admin dashboard to undeploy or delete any video at any time. Deleting removes the video from both the local database and the Gawain CDN.

== External Service ==

This plugin relies on the Gawain AI Video Generation API hosted at gawain.nogeass.com to generate promotional videos.

**No data is sent to any external server until the site administrator explicitly enables "External processing" in the plugin settings.**

When enabled, the following data is transmitted to gawain.nogeass.com upon user-initiated actions (Generate, Deploy, Undeploy, Delete):

* Product title (max 80 characters, HTML stripped)
* Product short description (max 200 characters, HTML stripped)
* Product image URL (one image)
* Product price and currency
* Site hostname (used as installation identifier)

**Purpose:** AI video generation and storefront video delivery (CDN).

**Data retention:** Generated videos are stored on Gawain CDN servers. Deleting a video through the plugin dashboard removes it from both the local database and the remote server.

**Third-party sharing:** Product data is not shared with any third party beyond the Gawain service itself.
